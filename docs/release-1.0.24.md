# OpenClam Studio 1.0.24 / iOS 1.0.5 (73)

Two-finger swipes now rotate the macOS 3D avatar in the direction of the fingers in both chat and avatar mode. Approach and retreat no longer stop and turn halfway: crown framing hands over continuously, and the camera adjustment from a saved placement no longer changes walking direction or cadence. Close views keep the head near the top edge while the lower body leaves below.

This update also includes reliable motion delivery for revised and batched Live Talk replies, conversation-driven actions when automatic reactions are off, motion containment within the available view, and reduced iPhone rendering and bridge work. The original Tia geometry and hair are unchanged.

Validation includes repeated approach/retreat at 30 and 60 FPS across phone, chat and desktop sizes, saved placement offsets, swipe/drag/pinch ownership, all 62 Tia motion rig checks, and iOS unit and simulator interaction tests. Physical iPhone performance has not been measured in this release cycle.
